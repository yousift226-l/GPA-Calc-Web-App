About

What is it?

GPA-Calc is a site where students can calculate their GPA through the built-in calculator on the "GPA Calculator" page. All it takes is a simple input of grade letters and credits to get an accurate GPA instantly. The AI API is a chatbot where any inquiries about education/gpa will be delivered.

Who is it for?

This tool is built for students who want a fast, no-hassle way of calculating their cumulative GPA and questions that need to be answered.

Why was it created?

This website was designed to be an easy-access of calculating a GPA. No sign-up and no questions, just input data and calculate. This project was also created as a progression of learning front-end development.
